Facts and numbers I used

  • 56,000 reports, 2005 to 2009. From the FDA's Infusion Pump Improvement Initiative white paper. These are adverse event reports, not confirmed injuries.
  • "One program does it all" is a simplified picture of a non-real-time design, not a claim about any brand. Many real pumps already have their own alarms and detectors. The voiceover says "if", on purpose.
  • 10 ms and 3 ms are our averages from QNX 8.0 in QEMU (9.7 ms for a blocked line, 3.1 ms for air), rounded.
  • 100 of 100 is our bench: 5 faults, 20 runs each, none missed.
  • The drug, the 50 mL/h rate and the IV bag are a simulated demo, not a real patient setup.
